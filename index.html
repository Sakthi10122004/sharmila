Namba Munna Maari Pesuradhey Illa...

Munna yellam evlo neram pesuvom ma... neraiya pesitey irupom la? Ippo suthama pesuradhu illa. Morning, afternoon, night — call panni "Saapittiya?" nu kekkadha naal irukka? Illa.

Sorry ma... apdi nadandhukittadhukku. En mela thaan thappu.

02

En Aasaiyum En Bayamum...

Un kooda irundhu dress iron panni, pack panni kuduthu, chicken saaptu... un kooda konjam neram jolly-ah irukkanum nu thaan nenachaen. Sudden-ah nee "Venaam po" nu sonna odaney nenjey oru maari aaiduchi.

Romba bayama irukku amma, nee vittu poiduviyo nu...

Enakku un mela irukkura care — yaarumey kuduthurukka maatanga.

03

Naan Potta Effort... Yaarum Panna Maattanga

Naan potta effort, naan kaattina anbu yaarum kaatta maattanga. Aana nee ennaiya un maari 1% kooda treat panradhu illa ma.

Nee kooda irundha mattum podhum... nee enakku avlo periya varam.

04

Nee Call Pannumbodhellaam...

Ippo naan evlo moora call pannaalum edukkaradhu illa. WhatsApp-la message send panna — reply vidu, paakkardhu kooda illa. Enakku evlo kashtama irukkum theriyuma?

Maha, Nishanth kitta jolly-ah irukkura maari en kitta irukka maattiya nu oru yekkam enakku irukkum dhi...

05

En Pazhaya Innocent Sharmila Venum...

Ellathaiyum en kitta kekkanum, naan sollitharanum — "Pah naan ippadi pannaen, enakku credits kedachadhu!" nu solluviye... antha innocent Sharmila thaan enakku venum.

Neeye "sera maattom" nu sollum bodhu — en idhayamey vedichidum pola irukku 😭.

06

Yen Odanae Reply Panraen?

Of course — nee enakku priority thaan. And avlo yekkathula irukkaen nee call pannuva nu... Jolly-ah pesuna adhey podhum enakku.

Nee illadha naatkal romba kashtama irukku ma...
